Put BKGFFL on Your Home Screen

60 seconds. 4 taps. Then the Ledger lives on your phone like a real app — no more digging through text messages for the link.

BK Start Here Add BKGFFL to your phone ↓
iPhone · Safari or Chrome
1
Hit the Share button

Top-right corner of the address bar — the square icon with the up-arrow. Tap it.

2
Tap the down arrow (Chrome only)

The share menu pops up. On Chrome you'll see View More with a down-arrow ▾ in the bottom right of the top row. Tap it to expand the full menu.

If you're using Safari, skip this step — scroll the menu and go straight to step 3.
3
Tap Add to Home Screen

Scroll until you see it. The icon is a plus sign ⊕ inside a square. Tap it.

4
Tap Add

A little confirmation shows the name BKGFFL and the icon. Leave it as-is and hit Add in the top-right corner.

You're In
Check your home screen.

A wine-red BK icon is waiting. Tap it — the Ledger opens full-screen, no browser bars, just the receipts.
